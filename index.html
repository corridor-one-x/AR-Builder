Privacy Policy

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to share through the enquiry form on our Contact page. This policy explains what that is, why we ask for it and how we look after it.

01

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Project type and location
  • Estimated budget (optional)
  • The project details you write

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics profiling or advertising pixels, and we do not collect any information from you when you simply browse the site.

02

Why we collect it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry: to contact you, understand your project, prepare an estimate and arrange a site visit or meeting if you ask for one.

03

How we store it

Form submissions are delivered to our business email inbox and are accessible only to the AR Builder team handling your enquiry. We take reasonable measures to keep this information secure.

04

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the duration of the project and any legal record-keeping period that follows. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 12 months.

05

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We will not share it with third parties except where required by law or, with your consent, with a consultant or partner directly involved in your project.

06

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to view, correct or delete the details you have sent us. Just get in touch using the contact information below and we will act on it promptly.

07

Third-party links

Our site links to Instagram and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

08
